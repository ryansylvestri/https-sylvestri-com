## Foreclosure is a timing problem first

Most homeowners wait too long because the situation feels emotionally loaded and legally confusing. The practical move is to understand the timeline and identify which options are still open before the auction window closes in.

## Options before the worst-case outcome

- Sell voluntarily and preserve as much equity as possible.
- Explore modification or negotiated workout paths when the numbers support it.
- Understand whether a short sale is actually viable before spending energy on it.
