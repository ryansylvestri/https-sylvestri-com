# Copy System

Date: 2026-03-24
Site: `sylvestri.com`

## Goal

Turn `sylvestri.com` into Ryan Sylvestri's personal-brand hub with clear routing into:

- buyers
- sellers
- investors
- renters / relocation
- AI / systems

The site should convert by matching the visitor to the right lane quickly, not by forcing everyone through one generic brokerage message.

## Core Rules

1. One page, one promise, one CTA.
2. Lead with the visitor's situation before talking about Ryan.
3. Put trust, proof, and privacy close to the form.
4. Keep first-touch forms short and gather more detail after the lead raises a hand.
5. Use local specificity: Hudson Valley, key towns, commute logic, timing, and market fit.
6. Use direct language over polished real-estate filler.

## Message Hierarchy

### Homepage

- Function: route people fast
- Promise: one name, clear next steps
- CTA: `Start here`
- Secondary CTA: `Read the story`

### Buyers

- Promise: clarity before wasted weekends
- Lead ideas:
  - budget and financing clarity
  - town fit
  - faster offer readiness

### Sellers

- Promise: value, timing, and net clarity
- Lead ideas:
  - what the home could sell for
  - what it could net
  - whether to sell now, prep first, or hold

### Investors

- Promise: cleaner underwriting and faster screening
- Lead ideas:
  - criteria-first intake
  - condition and downside awareness
  - strategy-specific follow-up

### Renters / Relocation

- Promise: reduce uncertainty fast
- Lead ideas:
  - move date
  - target towns
  - rental now versus future-buy path

### AI / Systems

- Promise: applied leverage, not abstract theory
- Lead ideas:
  - workflow bottlenecks
  - automation opportunities
  - operator-grade implementation

## Landing Pages

Landing pages should convert deeper intent. They should:

- identify one specific situation
- explain one strong reason to act now
- offer one clean next step
- route into the shared intake or a tighter follow-up page

High-priority landing families:

- home valuation
- seller launch plan
- first-time buyers
- relocation
- investor intake
- motivated-seller situations

## Squeeze Pages

Squeeze pages should convert lighter intent. They should:

- stay short
- offer one immediate value exchange
- move the lead into a deeper landing page after opt-in

Best use cases:

- seller curiosity
- buyer alerts
- move guides
- investor criteria
- direct-response "realist" offers

## Research Notes

The rewrite was guided by current conversion patterns from:

- NAR buyer and seller profile coverage
- Follow Up Boss landing-page guidance
- HubSpot landing-page copy guidance
- Unbounce conversion best practices

Applied conclusions:

- buyers respond to clarity, not hype
- sellers respond to valuation, equity, and timing
- investors respond to speed and numbers
- relocation leads respond to town-fit orientation
- motivated sellers respond to calm, private, options-based language
